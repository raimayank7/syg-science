Photosynthesis: how plants make food

How a green leaf turns sunlight, water and air into food and gives out oxygen.

Classes 3 to 5Biology361 words

Animals have to find their food. Green plants do something no animal can do: they make their own food. The process is called photosynthesis. The word comes from "photo", meaning light, and "synthesis", meaning putting together. So photosynthesis means "putting together with the help of light".

What a plant needs

  • Sunlight, which gives the energy.
  • Water, which the roots take in from the soil and the stem carries up to the leaves.
  • Carbon dioxide, a gas in the air that enters the leaf through tiny openings called stomata.
  • Chlorophyll, the green substance in the leaf that traps sunlight.

What happens in the leaf

Inside the leaf, chlorophyll captures the energy of sunlight. The plant uses this energy to join water and carbon dioxide together to make a kind of sugar called glucose. This sugar is the plant's food. During the process, oxygen is released into the air through the stomata.

Carbon dioxide + Water → (sunlight, chlorophyll) → Glucose + Oxygen

What happens to the food

The plant uses some of the glucose at once for energy and growth. The rest is changed into starch and stored in leaves, roots, stems, fruits and seeds. When we eat a potato, rice or a mango, we are eating food that a plant stored. Even when we eat an egg or drink milk, the hen and the cow got their food from plants. In this way almost all food on earth can be traced back to photosynthesis.

Why photosynthesis matters to us

  • It makes food for plants, and through them for all animals and people.
  • It gives out oxygen, the gas that we and other animals need to breathe.
  • It uses up carbon dioxide, which helps to keep the air balanced.

This is why cutting down forests harms everyone, and why planting trees helps.

Common doubts

Do plants make food at night? No. Photosynthesis needs light, so it stops in the dark. Do plants breathe? Yes. Plants respire all day and night, like animals, using a little oxygen. But during the day they give out far more oxygen than they use. What about plants with red or purple leaves? They also have chlorophyll; other colours hide the green.

Key terms

Photosynthesis
making food from carbon dioxide and water using sunlight
Chlorophyll
green substance in leaves that traps light
Glucose
the simple sugar a plant makes as food
Starch
the form in which plants store food
Stomata
tiny openings in a leaf

Try it yourself

Cover one leaf of a potted plant on both sides with black paper and clip it. Leave the plant in sunlight for three or four days, then remove the paper. The covered leaf looks paler than the others because it could not get light.

Quick questions

Answer in your head or on paper first, then tap to check.

Name the four things a plant needs for photosynthesis.

Sunlight, water, carbon dioxide and chlorophyll.

Which gas is released during photosynthesis?

Oxygen.

In what form do plants store food?

As starch.

Why can photosynthesis not happen at night?

Because it needs light.
